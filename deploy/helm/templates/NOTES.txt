{{- if .Values.authorizationPolicy.enabled }}

The Istio AuthorizationPolicy needs a sidecar on the API pods and on every caller, with mutual TLS
between them: the mesh names each caller by its service account, so a caller without a mesh identity
is refused on the internal paths (/api/v1/internal/*). Enforcing mutual TLS mesh-wide
(PeerAuthentication) is Platform's, not this chart's. The caller service accounts and namespaces in
authorizationPolicy.internalRoutes and networkPolicy.allowedCallers are placeholders to confirm with Platform.
{{- else }}

authorizationPolicy.enabled=false: only the NetworkPolicy stands in front of the internal routes, and
any pod it admits can send the x-user-id and x-tenant-roles headers the service checks (they are not a
credential). The Istio policy needs a sidecar and mutual TLS; PeerAuthentication is Platform's.
{{- end }}
